# Cosmic Brew: Game Design Document

| | |
|---|---|
| **Genre** | Relaxing management / cozy game |
| **Platforms** | PC (Windows), Web planned |
| **Visual style** | Low-poly 3D, pastel palette, lo-fi aesthetics |
| **Engine** | Unity 6 (URP) |

---

## 1. Overview

*Cosmic Brew* is a 3D management game built entirely around atmosphere and relaxation. The player controls a retired (and very tired) worker robot who opened a tiny floating coffee kiosk in the middle of the cosmos. There are no timers, no penalties and no angry customers. The goal is to enjoy the vibe, brew intergalactic coffee at your own pace, and spend the profits making the kiosk cozier.

## 2. Story and World

The universe is a busy place, full of cargo ships, star wars and black holes. The kiosk, however, sits on a quiet "rest route".

The hero is **R0-B0 ("Ro")**, a robot who used to work in heavy mining and saved up for a peaceful life. Customers are space travelers (friendly aliens, lost astronauts and other machines) who stop by because they need a break, not because they are in a hurry. The soundtrack of the world is a lo-fi radio that echoes through the void.

## 3. Core Loop

The loop is designed to be continuous and meditative:

1. **Welcome:** a customer arrives by ship and places an order in a cute thought bubble.
2. **Prepare:** Ro walks to the stations (coffee machine, stardust grinder, nebula milk steamer) to assemble the drink. **There is no time limit.**
3. **Serve and watch:** the player hands over the drink. The customer takes a sip, lets out a relaxed *"Ahhh…"* and tips in **Star Coins**.
4. **Expand:** coins are spent in the catalog on decorations, colors and new drink stations.

## 4. Aesthetics and Vibe

- **Colors:** a space backdrop in deep purple, navy and pastel pink, with no pure blacks. The kiosk lights are warm (orange, soft yellow) to contrast with cold space.
- **Camera:** an isometric (diagonal) view with smooth-damped movement.
- **Audio:** the heart of the game. A continuous lo-fi playlist and satisfying ASMR sound effects: the coffee machine, clinking cups, ships humming by.
- **Character:** Ro's animations convey "cozy tiredness": slightly slumped shoulders, happy digital eyes.

## 5. Core Mechanics

- **Zen orders:** orders float above each customer. A wrong ingredient is never a failure: dump the cup in the bin and try again. Customers never leave upset; they just bob their heads to the music.
- **Grid-based decoration:** in Build Mode the player buys, moves, rotates, paints and stores decorations, recolors the island and buys new drink stations.
- **Interactive radio:** a physical radio on the kiosk that switches the current lo-fi track.
